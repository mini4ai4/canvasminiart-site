About

I am Hana, and this shop is built around one idea: Japanese ink art and the calm it brings to a wall. Deep black ink, a wide field of white, and sometimes a single quiet accent of red.

Every design here is made in that spirit, in the sumi-e tradition of the fewest possible marks. You can take a piece as an instant digital download and print it yourself, or order it printed for you on museum-grade matte paper, shipped rolled or framed and ready to hang.

Not sure which size fits your wall? Message me on Etsy with a photo of the space and I will tell you honestly which size works.
