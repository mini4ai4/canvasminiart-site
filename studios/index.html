Ink for quiet rooms

Black sumi ink on warm paper, one stroke of vermilion. Painted for spaces where someone is meant to slow their breathing — a treatment room, a studio, the chair where they wait.

Why this work belongs on a treatment-room wall

A room where people lie down and close their eyes cannot carry a busy picture. These paintings are mostly empty paper. The eye rests, finds one dark shape and one red mark, and stops looking.

Each piece is an ink-wash painting in the sumi-e tradition — the discipline of the fewest possible marks. Nothing photographic, nothing loud, nothing that dates.
